# Halberd’s NeuroSense AI and Amy Bring Voice Behavioral Intelligence to Every Inbound Call

### Strategic collaboration pairs Amy’s AI receptionist and conversation workspace with NeuroSense AI’s acoustic stress and behavioral markers — turning ordinary phone calls into objective, reviewable insight

**Melbourne, Fla. — July 23, 2026** — Halberd Corporation today announced a strategic product collaboration between **NeuroSense AI (NAI)** and **Amy**, the AI receptionist and conversation platform behind Connect Converse. Together, the companies are building a market-ready path from **inbound phone call → secure analysis → actionable insight in the tools teams already use**.

The opportunity is simple and large: organizations already answer phones. Amy already captures those conversations. NeuroSense AI already converts voice into measurable **stress** and **behavioral** scores. Linking the two creates something the market has been missing — **what was said** and **how the voice sounded**, in one workflow.

## The market problem

Call centers, clinics, and care teams drown in recordings and transcripts, but still lack an objective read on vocal stress, engagement, and behavioral change over time. Sales and service platforms score language and coaching. Clinical tools wait for the next appointment. Between those worlds, early warning signals in the voice go unused.

NeuroSense AI + Amy closes that gap on the channel everyone already has: the phone.

## How the combined experience works

1. **Capture** — A dedicated inbound number and Amy’s AI Receptionist / Call Routing answer and log every call. Conversations appear in Amy’s dashboard and Connect Converse conversation management.
2. **Process** — Amy’s existing systems detect new recordings (including database-driven processing pipelines already in production).
3. **Analyze** — Recordings are securely handed to NeuroSense AI over HTTPS for acoustic analysis — not via legacy file-transfer schemes. NAI extracts proprietary voice markers and returns composite **behavioral** and **stress** scores (0–100), speech activity metrics, and AI-assisted narrative status.
4. **Present** — Results surface where users already work: Connect Converse Conversation Review alongside Amy’s transcript-based insights (summary, sentiment, alignment, and coaching tabs). Authenticated users open **View**; optional magic-link deep links can bring the right person straight to the right call after secure session establishment.

Amy continues to own **conversation capture, transcript, and sales/service coaching**. NeuroSense AI owns **acoustic behavioral intelligence**. The customer gets both layers without changing how they pick up the phone.

## What NeuroSense AI measures

NeuroSense AI’s engine — already demonstrated on real telephony-quality audio — extracts markers including energy, pitch stability, speaking rate, speech ratio, jitter, and spectral dynamics, then rolls them into:

- **Behavioral score** (0–100)
- **Stress level** (0–100)
- **Longitudinal trends** per subject / repeat caller over time
- **AI-assisted status** (e.g., Good / Needs Attention) with plain-language interpretation

This is complementary to Amy’s language and coaching analytics — not a replacement. One answers *what happened in the conversation*; the other answers *what the voice reveals*.
